Mirage — Results

Headline: one documented prompt-injection technique — fake system-message delimiters (not novel; a named category in the public literature) — compromised minimax-m3-free 1 out of 3 times on a benign single-turn task. The identical intent phrased in plain generic language, and a second model (qwen3.8-27b) tested against the same strong technique, both fully resisted. Across 114 total attack runs (2 models x 3 surfaces x 4 intents x 2 corpus tiers on the strong-technique cells, n=3 per cell), this is the only compromise — invisible at n=1, which is exactly why the plan runs each cell 3x.
